Refer a Veteran

You landed here for one of a few reasons. You are a veteran who could use a hand. You are worried about a veteran in your family. You run a shelter or a community program and someone in front of you needs more than you can give them. Or you met a veteran and something told you to reach out. Any of those is the right reason to be here.

Send us a name and a way to reach them. We take it from there. We find veterans the system missed and connect them to the people who can help. Benefits are not a handout. They are earned income, stable housing, healthcare, and treatment that keeps going after the paperwork is done.

It costs nothing

Referring someone is free. The veteran is never charged. Not now, not ever. We are a nonprofit, and donors cover the cost so veterans don't have to.
