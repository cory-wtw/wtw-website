Why this matters

The stakes

In Tennessee tonight, an estimated 570 veterans will sleep without a home. Many earned VA disability benefits they've never received, monthly tax-free compensation with an estimated lifetime value around $549,000. No one ever showed them how to claim it. We find them, and connect them to the people who can help.

VA 2024 Point-in-Time Count; 2026 VA compensation rates. Lifetime value based on an observed FY2025 average of approximately $27,461 per year per compensated veteran, projected over twenty years without cost-of-living adjustment. Individual outcomes vary widely by rating and circumstance.

Donors pay so veterans don't.

Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit funded entirely by donors, sponsors, and grants. We go out and find veterans and connect each one to the organizations that can help. When a VA claim is involved, we connect them to a VA-accredited VSO partner. We are not a law firm and we don't charge veterans a cent. Your partnership is what makes that possible.
