Our model

Donors pay so veterans don't.

Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) funded entirely by donors, sponsors, and grants. We find veterans and connect each one to the organizations that can help. When a VA claim is involved, we connect them to a VA-accredited VSO partner. We're not a law firm, and we never charge veterans a cent. Your partnership is what makes that possible.
