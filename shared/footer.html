Free for veterans. Always. No fees, no dues, no catch, ever. Funded by donors, sponsors, and grants.

Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. EIN 41-5275144. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Registered with the Tennessee Secretary of State as required by law (No. CO54883).

Worth Their Weight is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation before the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. All claims-related services are performed by VA-accredited attorneys, agents, or Veterans Service Organizations.
