The Mission

They earned it.
We help them get to it.

A veteran rarely shows up with one problem — housing, a job, an ID, benefits, somewhere to sleep tonight, all at once. Most organizations solve one and hand over a list for the rest. We do the sorting.

Tell us what's going on; fifteen minutes is usually enough. We work out what you need — from housing and health care to benefits, work, and legal — then email you the organizations that fit, each with a name and a number. Two weeks later we check in, and if a door didn't open, we find the next one.

Any veteran. Any era. Any discharge. No cost.

In the Field

Operation Search & Rescue

Some veterans are never going to call. So we go find them.

We embed in the community — shelters, local events, and the relationships that lead us to the veterans the system missed. No clipboard, no clinical lens. Real human contact first.

Once we find someone, the practical barriers come down before anything else. A ride to an appointment. A phone and a way to get online. A week somewhere safe while things get sorted. Then the work of connecting them begins.

Worth Their Weight prioritizes unsheltered veterans first, the population the desk-based referral model cannot reach. We do not turn away veterans who walk in or are referred to us. Triage is based on need.
