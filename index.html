How it works

One conversation. The right people, by name.

A veteran rarely shows up with one problem: housing, a job, an ID, benefits, somewhere to sleep tonight, all at once. Most organizations solve one and hand over a list for the rest. We do the sorting.

Any veteran. Any era. Any discharge. No cost.

In the field

Operation Search & Rescue

Some veterans are never going to call. So we go find them. Shelters, local events, and the relationships that lead us to the veterans the system missed. No clipboard. Real human contact first.

A ride

To the appointment that's been missed three times.

A phone

And a way to get online, so help can actually reach them.

A safe place

A week somewhere safe while the bigger things get sorted.

We prioritize unsheltered veterans first, the people a desk-based referral model can't reach. We don't turn away veterans who walk in or are referred to us. Triage is based on need.
