In the field

Operation Search & Rescue

Some veterans are never going to call. So we go find them. Shelters, local events, and the relationships that lead us to the veterans the system missed. No clipboard. Real human contact first.

A safe place

A week somewhere safe while the bigger things get sorted.

We prioritize unsheltered veterans first, the people a desk-based referral model can't reach. We don't turn away veterans who walk in or are referred to us. Triage is based on need.

Care packs

What's a care pack?

A gallon bag with warm socks, hand warmers, snacks, hygiene basics and a wallet card with our number.

Who gets one?

Any unsheltered neighbor. No questions asked.

How do they get there?

Local packers build them. We deliver them to shelters that already reach people living outside. We're starting in Chattanooga.

Why?

Some of those neighbors are veterans the system missed. The card asks one question: “Are you a veteran?” From there we connect them to benefits they may qualify for. Free, always.
