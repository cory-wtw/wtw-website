FAQ

Before you lace up

What do I carry?

Any backpack. Weight optional. This is non-competitive.

Where do I park?

Limited parking at Blue Goose Hollow. Use Ross's Landing or downtown lots, a short walk up the Riverwalk.

Is it really free?

Yes. Registration is free for everyone. Instead of an entry fee, we ask each participant to fundraise, gathering pledges by the mile from friends and family. Every dollar goes to finding veterans the system missed.

Can we participate as a team?

Yes. Start or join a team when you register. Families, workplaces, gyms, and veteran groups all fundraise better together, and there's a team leaderboard and team award.

Where does the money go?

Every dollar helps us find unsheltered veterans and connect them to the benefits they've earned, starting right here in Chattanooga.
