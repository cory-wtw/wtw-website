Why this matters

The stakes

In Tennessee tonight, an estimated 570 veterans will sleep without a home. Many earned VA disability benefits they've never received, monthly tax-free compensation with an estimated lifetime value around $549,000. No one ever showed them how to claim it. We find them, and connect them to the people who can help.

Donors pay so veterans don't.

Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit funded entirely by donors, sponsors, and grants. We go out and find veterans and connect each one to the organizations that can help. When a VA claim is involved, we connect them to a VA-accredited VSO partner. We are not a law firm and we don't charge veterans a cent. Your gift is what makes that possible.

Common Questions

Good to know

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, EIN 41-5275144. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Where does my money actually go?

Straight to outreach and follow-through: finding veterans, connecting them to the right organizations, and covering the gap while a claim or a placement comes through. Donors pay so veterans never do.

Do you take donations other than online giving?

Yes — checks, in-kind gifts, and company sponsorships all count. Call or text 833-WTW-VETS (833-989-8387) to talk it through.
