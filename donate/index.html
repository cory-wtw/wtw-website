Our model

Donors pay so veterans don't.

Worth Their Weight is funded entirely by donors, sponsors, and grants. We find veterans and connect each one to the organizations that can help. When a VA claim is involved, we connect them to a VA-accredited VSO partner. We're not a law firm, and we never charge veterans a cent.

Common questions

Good to know

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Worth Their Weight is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, EIN 41-5275144. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Where does my money actually go?

Straight to outreach and follow-through: finding veterans, connecting them to the right organizations, and covering the gap while a claim or a placement comes through. Donors pay so veterans never do.

Do you take donations other than online giving?

Yes. Checks and in-kind gifts both count. Call or text 833-989-8387 to talk it through.

Can my company sponsor you?

Yes, and thank you. Call or text 833-989-8387 and we'll talk it through.
