What's in a care pack

A gallon bag with warm socks, hand warmers, snacks, hygiene basics and a wallet card with our number.

How it works

Who gets one?

Any unsheltered neighbor. No questions asked.

How do they get there?

Local packers build them. We deliver them to shelters that already reach people living outside. We're starting in Chattanooga.

Why?

Some of those neighbors are veterans the system missed. The card asks one question: “Are you a veteran?” From there we connect them to benefits they may qualify for. Free, always.
