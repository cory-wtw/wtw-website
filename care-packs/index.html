What's in a care pack

A gallon bag with warm socks, hand warmers, snacks, hygiene basics and a postcard with our number.

How it works

Who gets one?

Any unsheltered neighbor. No questions asked.

How do they get there?

We pack them and deliver them to shelters that already reach people living outside. We're starting in Chattanooga.

Why?

Some of those neighbors are veterans the system missed. The postcard asks one question: “Did you serve in the military?” From there we connect veterans and their families to benefits they may qualify for. Free, always.
