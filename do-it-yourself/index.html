Filing it yourself? Here are the steps.

1

Watch the VA's short video

The VA explains how a disability claim works.

Watch the VA's video (YouTube)
2

Write down your health problems

Write down each health problem you have and about when it started. Add where you served and what you did. This list is just to help you remember.

The VA explains what it covers on its own pages. It decides what qualifies.

What the VA covers Exposures in service
3

Get your DD-214

Your DD-214 is your discharge paper. Ask for the long form, called Member Copy 4. It is the full copy, and it shows the type of discharge the VA asks about. It is free. Never pay a website for your records.

First check your own papers and your county clerk. No luck? Mail or fax a form called the SF-180 to the National Archives. Call us and we'll mail you one. You can also request it online.

SF-180 form (VA page) Request your DD-214 online (National Archives)
4

Read about the intent to file

An intent to file may let benefits start from an earlier date, if the claim is filed within a year. The VA's page explains how.

Read the VA's intent to file page
5

File your claim

The VA's form is called VA Form 21-526EZ. You can file it online or mail it in. The VA's page shows you how.

Have these ready:

  • Your DD-214
  • Your notes from step 2
  • Any medical records you have
  • A list of your medicines
Get VA Form 21-526EZ Read the VA's how to file page

Rather use the phone? Call the VA at 800-827-1000 and ask them to mail you the form.

6

After you file

The wait is normal. Check on your claim by calling the VA at 800-827-1000.

If the VA schedules an exam, go to it. If you can't make it, call the VA right away to reschedule.

Disagree with the decision? The letter explains your options and deadlines. A free VSO can help if you want.

Read the VA's page on reviews

Rather have someone do it with you?

That's fine too. A VSO is a helper the VA has accredited. Their help is free, and they can file the claim with you. The VA's search also lists lawyers and claims agents, who may charge. A VSO is the free choice.
